Add a compound

SMILES tips: write the Lewis structure you want students to see. Hydrogens are added automatically. Expanded octets are written with their bonds (O=S=O, FS(F)(F)(F)(F)F). Put Si and Xe in brackets ([SiH4], F[Xe]F). Mark cis/trans with slashes: Cl/C=C\Cl is cis, Cl/C=C/Cl is trans.

Bond moments (arrow lengths)

Bond-dipole arrows are drawn to scale from these values. Rows marked estimate are placeholders where no experimental bond moment is available. Edit the value and source, then press Save.
